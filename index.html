HOW IT WORKS

Connecting Explorers with Brands

MessageHunt creates a real-world bridge between curious explorers and business creators. Explorers use the mobile app to collect rewards, while creators use the Web Portal to purchase coins and drop promotional campaigns targeted to specific Indian states.

1. Explorers Download App

Users download MessageHunt on Play Store or App Store to explore their city map and collect real coin rewards within a 111m radius.

2. Creators Buy Coins

Businesses & creators sign in on this web portal, request coin packages via email, and receive coin credits from the admin team.

3. Drop & Promote Across India

Creators drop coins with attached URLs and banners across target Indian states. Explorers visit coordinates to claim and engage!
